The Perfect Fiery Companion

Gamtu's Red Chilly Sauce is crafted from fresh, sun-dried whole red chillies sourced directly from farms in Guntur and Byadagi — the two most prized chilli-growing regions of India. The chillies are cleaned, sun-dried, and stone-ground with a blend of natural spices to create a sauce that is authentically fiery, deeply flavourful, and beautifully red — without a drop of artificial colour.

Unlike commercial sauces that rely on processed chilli extracts, our sauce uses whole-ground chillies, which means you get the full spectrum of flavour — not just heat. Perfect for Indo-Chinese cooking, as a dipping sauce for momos and spring rolls, or as a table condiment.

  • Made from Guntur + Byadagi sun-dried whole chillies
  • Zero MSG, zero preservatives, zero artificial colour
  • Stone-ground for a coarser, authentic texture
  • Medium-hot heat level — flavourful, not punishing
  • Ideal for Indo-Chinese, momos, noodles, and snacks

Momo & Dumpling Dipping SauceMix with a touch of sesame oil and garlic for the ultimate momo dipping sauce. The natural chilli flavour complements the steamed wrapper perfectly.
🍜
Indo-Chinese Noodles & Fried RiceAdd 2-3 tablespoons while stir-frying noodles or fried rice. The real chilli gives an authentic restaurant-quality Indo-Chinese flavour.
