Healthy Living,
The Traditional Way

From farms across India, organic, pure & Jain-friendly for everyday wellness

Gamtu is an Indian brand offering pure ready-to-eat jain food, herbal teas, copper bottles, and wellness products for those who want a healthy lifestyle.

We believe that traditional Indian habits like drinking water from copper, eating sattvic jain food, and choosing natural ingredients can still fit into today’s busy lifestyle.

With over 200 quality products, Gamtu brings together tradition, health, and convenience in one place.
