The Gold Standard of Ghee

Our Gir Cow Ghee is crafted from the milk of grass-fed, pasture-raised Gir cows using the traditional bilona method — milk is set into curd, hand-churned into butter, then slow-cooked into pure golden ghee.

The result is a ghee richer in CLA, Omega-3 fatty acids, fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K), and butyric acid — supporting gut health, reducing inflammation, and boosting immunity.

  • Made from 100% authentic A2 milk — no A1 cow mixing
  • No preservatives, additives, or artificial colours
  • Grainy texture confirms handmade, authentic process
  • Beautiful golden colour with natural nutty aroma
  • Satvik, Jain-friendly, and suitable for all diet types
  • Shelf-stable for 12 months without refrigeration

How to Use Gir Cow Ghee

🍳
For Cooking & TadkaUse 1-2 teaspoons for tempering spices, sautéing vegetables, or drizzling over dal. High smoke point (485°F) makes it ideal for frying.
🫓
Spread on Rotis & ParathasApply a generous layer on hot roti or paratha. The warmth melts the ghee perfectly, releasing its nutty aroma.
🙏
Ayurvedic Daily UseConsume ½ teaspoon on empty stomach in the morning with warm water for gut health and immunity boost.
📦
StorageStore at room temperature away from direct sunlight. No refrigeration needed. Shelf life: 12 months. Always use a dry, clean spoon.
